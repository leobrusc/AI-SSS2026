## Known limitations

No Linux A100 campaign ran locally. CUDA installation/runtime compatibility,
actual model loading and inference on the target driver remain to be verified by
the colleague. Linux wheel resolution is validated, not target GPU execution.
The original fault procedure must yield exactly six observations or validation
fails. Native circuit_half_open does not prove a distinct HALF_OPEN state.
No matched security overhead or cross-GPU equivalence claim is established.
No manuscript source was supplied; no manuscript was edited or certified.
RQ4 remains pending until real returned A100 evidence is independently audited.
