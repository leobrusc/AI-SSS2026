\paragraph{RQ2 (Provenance Gates)}
The 30-instance security campaign produces 240~observations across 8~scenarios. All scenarios achieve correct-decision rate~1.000 (Wilson 95\% CI: [0.886,\,1.000]). Zero false positives; zero false negatives. Gate latency: P50=34.2\,ms, P95=102.0\,ms, P99=138.8\,ms. Each instance uses an independent seed producing distinct mutation offsets in model weights, tokenizer files, and dataset manifests.

\paragraph{RQ3 (Scanner Agreement and VEX)}
Scanning a controlled fixture SBOM (6~packages with known vulnerabilities), Trivy identifies 54~findings and Grype identifies 35~findings. Raw-identifier Jaccard similarity: 0.047 (4~findings in intersection). The low agreement is primarily attributable to advisory identifier divergence: Trivy reports CVE identifiers while Grype reports GHSA identifiers for overlapping vulnerabilities. VEX policy conformance: 8/8 deterministic scenarios correct. Property-based testing: 500~generated cases with 100\% oracle agreement and 0~counterexamples. These measure policy conformance, not ecosystem-wide VEX effectiveness.
